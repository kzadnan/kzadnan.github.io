Research summary

Ph.D. candidate in mechanical engineering working on computational interfacial thermal transport. I use density-functional theory, machine-learning interatomic potentials, and nonequilibrium molecular dynamics on multi-GPU HPC systems to predict thermal boundary conductance in wide- and ultrawide-bandgap semiconductor heterostructures. Five peer-reviewed papers (four first-author) in Physical Review B, Physical Review Applied, Applied Physics Letters, and the International Journal of Heat and Mass Transfer. Central result: thermal boundary conductance is not a property of a single interface, but depends on the nearby environment.
