About

Computational heat transfer at the nanoscale

I am a Ph.D. candidate at the University of Utah (Feng research group) studying how heat leaves a device when the bottleneck is the interfaces between materials, not the bulk crystal.

I train machine-learning interatomic potentials from DFT, then run large-scale NEMD on HPC clusters to predict thermal boundary conductance at first-principles accuracy — targeting electro-thermal design rules for wide- and ultrawide-bandgap electronics.

Research interests

Problems I am actively working on

01

Successive-interface phonon transport

A nearby interface filters phonon modes, raising TBC by 1–1.5×. I map the critical separation distance for Si/Ge and III-nitride stacks.

02

MLIPs for heterogeneous interfaces

DeePMD and MTP models trained on DFT forces for chemically abrupt and mixed interfaces — driving NEMD at lengths inaccessible to direct DFT.

03

Interlayer & composition engineering

1 nm SiNx doubles Si/diamond TBC; a-C raises it to ~350 MW/m²K. AlGaN interlayers at GaN/AlN degrade TBC via alloy scattering.

04

WBG / UWBG thermal management

Quantifying TBC for GaN/AlN, GaN/diamond, Si/diamond, and metal/diamond contacts relevant to high-power RF and AI accelerators.

05

Scientific machine learning

Autoencoder–DMD phonon mode extraction, harmonic MLIPs in JAX, and grey-model MFP estimates for amorphous interlayers.

06

HPC simulation workflows

End-to-end pipelines: structure generation → DFT labeling → MLIP training → NEMD TBC extraction on multi-GPU clusters with active learning.

    Significant impact of Al1−xGaxN interlayer on GaN/AlN thermal boundary conductance

    Khalid Zobaid Adnan, Hao Zhou, and Tianli Feng

    AlGaN interlayer reduces GaN/AlN TBC — alloy scattering beats phonon-bridge intuition. Sigmoidal composition grade recovers conductance.

  2. PRA
    Physical Review Applied 2026 First author

    Si/diamond thermal boundary conductance enhanced by SiNx and amorphous carbon interlayers

    Khalid Zobaid Adnan, Tanvirul Abedien, and Tianli Feng

    MLIP-driven NEMD: bare Si/diamond TBC = 130 MW/m²K. 1 nm SiNx doubles it to 275; a-C reaches 350.

    DOI Journal
  3. PRB
    Physical Review B 2025

    Thermal boundary conductance in standalone and nonstandalone GaN/AlN heterostructures

    Hao Zhou, Khalid Zobaid Adnan, Wyatt Allen Jones, and Tianli Feng

    Standalone GaN/AlN TBC ~600 MW/m²K at 300 K. A second nearby interface raises it to ~1000 via ballistic phonon filtering.

    DOI arXiv
  4. IJHMT

    Thermal boundary conductance of metal–diamond interfaces predicted by machine learning interatomic potentials

    Khalid Zobaid Adnan, Mahesh R. Neupane, and Tianli Feng

    MLIPs for Al, Mo, Zr, Au on diamond. TBC correlates with metal Debye temperature, not elastic modulus.

    DOI arXiv
  5. PRB
    Physical Review B 2024 First author 14 citations

    Thermal boundary conductance and thermal conductivity strongly depend on nearby environment

    Khalid Zobaid Adnan and Tianli Feng

    Adding a nearby interface raises Si/Ge TBC from 400 to 700 MW/m²K. Sandwiching Si between Ge yields apparent κ above bulk.

    DOI Journal
